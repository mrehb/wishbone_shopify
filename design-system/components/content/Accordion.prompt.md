# Accordion

The store publishes no FAQ, no fitting guidance and no warranty page today — and a repairable
product generates precisely those questions. This is where they live.

First item open by default; one open at a time. Answers are the same voice as everywhere else:
name the part, say the number, stop. *"The NEO battery is a 27+ hole lithium pack, 369 €, and it is
a user-replaceable part."*

Never use it to hide shipping costs or returns terms — a collapsed accordion is where trust goes to
die. Those go in plain sight, and the accordion carries the genuinely long-tail questions.
