# RangeStrip

Five models as five dotted silhouettes — the clearest answer to "what does Wishbone make?", and it
ships without waiting for photography.

Electric models carry a volt cluster where the drive sits, so manual and electric are legible at a
glance before anyone reads a word. Order is always ONE → TWO → THREE → NEO → EON: manual to
electric, cheapest to flagship.

A model with no published price shows `—` rather than being dropped from the strip. The range is
five; a strip of four tells the customer the story is incomplete.
