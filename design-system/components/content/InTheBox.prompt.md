# InTheBox

What actually arrives, itemised with quantities. Cheap to build and disproportionately effective on
a considered purchase — it also pre-empts a large share of "is the charger included?" support mail.

List accessories that are **not** included as a note beneath, linked to the accessory: the four
universal holders (ball & tee, scorecard, umbrella, drink) are separate purchases and saying so
plainly sells them better than silence.
