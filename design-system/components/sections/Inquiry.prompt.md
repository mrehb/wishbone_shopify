# Inquiry

The form band from the reference. Four columns of copy on the left; on the right a two-column grid of underlined fields — first name, last name, email, phone, then a full-width message — and a single ink button with an arrow, right-aligned. The newsletter variant is one email field and the same button. No boxes, no placeholders doing the label's job, no lime here: the lime button on a page is the purchase.
