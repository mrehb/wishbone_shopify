# Parts

Twenty-two of the store's twenty-seven products are spare parts, so this is the band that earns its keep. A row of model tags filters the grid; the count is written out (*14 parts fit the NEO*). Each part: its photograph, or its model's silhouette until one exists; the exact store title with the model in brackets; the models it fits as tags with the current filter lit lime; the price. Four across, mist tiles, nothing rounded.
