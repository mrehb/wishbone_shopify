# Feature

The workhorse band: five columns of text, seven of image, either way round. Three tones — paper, mist, and **ink**, which is the one dark band a page is allowed, used for the thing with the most technology in it (the battery, the drive train). On ink the eyebrow is lime text, the copy is light grey and the button is lime; on paper the button is outline. Up to three facts sit under the copy as value-over-label pairs (`369,00 €` / `battery`). No icons next to the facts — the reference's little glyphs become the number itself.
