# ColorwaySelector

The trolleys' only variant axis. Real handles from the catalogue: `charcoal-black`,
`charcoal-lime`, `charcoal-red`, `white-red` — ONE, TWO, THREE and EON each ship three of them.

Swatches are **split circles**, because the products are two-tone and a single dot misrepresents
the object. The selected ring is volt — one of the few places volt appears inside a form, and the
reason the selector needs no label to be understood.

`charcoal-lime` is the hardware green (≈`#B4FA6E`), not volt. Do not "correct" it to the brand
accent: the swatch must match the thing that arrives in the box.
