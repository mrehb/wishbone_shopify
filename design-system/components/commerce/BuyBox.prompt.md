# BuyBox

One primary action, full width, and nothing competing with it — this is the screen's single volt
element, so the price sits in white mono beside it rather than fighting for the accent.

Two facts close a trolley sale and both belong here: **when it arrives** and **whether parts will
exist in three years**. State the parts promise as a number — *"14 parts listed for the NEO"* — not
as a reassurance. It is true, checkable, and the reason a customer chooses a repairable trolley.

Sold out disables the button and changes its label to the reason. Never hide the price, never
substitute "contact us for pricing".
