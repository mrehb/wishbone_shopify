# SpecPanel

The block this rebrand was built for: a product's specifications reported as instrument panels
instead of a bullet list. Auto-numbers its stats `01…n` in reading order.

Use real measurements only. Fold size and weight are currently missing from the catalogue — until
they exist, those panels print `—`, which is honest and also a standing reminder to go and measure
them. A brand whose voice is "say the number" cannot fill gaps with adjectives.
