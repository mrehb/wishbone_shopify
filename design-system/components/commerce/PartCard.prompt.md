# PartCard

**15 of the 27 live products have no photograph** — every one of them a spare part. A grey
placeholder says "unfinished shop"; a dot-matrix plate says "instrument", carries the brand, and
keeps the grid scannable while photography is commissioned.

The plate is a fallback, not a style choice: **as soon as a part has a real photograph, it uses
it.** Compatibility chips do the work the image cannot — a customer scanning for a NEO wheel reads
the chip before the title.

Titles keep their `(MODEL)` suffix in full. Never trim it to fit two lines; shorten the card, not
the name.
