# CompatTile

Pick a model, see only the parts that fit, with a dot-bar of how many parts each model has
(ONE 10 · NEO 14 · EON 5 · TWO 2 · THREE 2 today). A customer arrives knowing their trolley, not a
part number — this tile is the catalogue's structure made usable.

Never hide a model with no parts. The empty state names the gap and offers the route: send us the
serial number. TWO and THREE currently return almost nothing, and the bar makes that visible.
