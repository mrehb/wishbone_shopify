# Header

Seventy-two pixels: the wordmark at 14px tall on the left, five 12px-caps links centred, `CART (n)` as text, and the page's one lime button on the right. A hairline beneath. It is paper by default; on a dark product page it may be ink, using the negative wordmark. No icons, no hamburger drawn — on mobile the nav becomes a `MENU` word.
