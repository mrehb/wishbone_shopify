# Silhouettes

The EON grid is sampled from its actual photograph — luminance becomes dot size, the green hardware
becomes lime. It is the real object, not a drawing of one. The other four models use a procedural
outline **only until they are photographed to §09 of the brand book**, at which point they are
sampled the same way and the outline is deleted.

Never hand-draw a product. Never re-use the EON grid for another model.
