# Button

A rectangle with no radius, 12px tracked caps, 14px by 24px padding, and a typographic arrow `→` when it leads somewhere. Four kinds: **lime** is the purchase and appears once per view; **ink** is the strong secondary on paper; **outline** is every other action, drawn in the current text colour so it works on paper and on the ink band alike; **text** is a bare label with an arrow. Disabled is the same button at 38% — no grey fill. No icons, no glow, no lift on hover; only colour changes.
