# Silhouette and Photo

`Photo` is the single image slot in the system: a mist rectangle at a given ratio holding the store's white-background photograph, multiplied onto the mist so the white disappears and the product appears to sit on the backdrop. When there is no photograph — fifteen of the twenty-two spare parts today — it draws the model's dot silhouette instead: the EON's sampled from its real photo, the others a procedural outline until they are photographed. On the ink band pass `tone="paper"`.
