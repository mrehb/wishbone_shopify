# Pill

The single rounded element. A **button** is a lime pill; a **ghost** is an outlined pill; a **tag**
is a grey chip; a **status** chip carries a dot (grey idle, lime live); a **select** pill is a
radio for models and colourways. Five kinds, one shape.

Lime fill always carries ground-coloured text. One lime button per tile at most, and the one
lime button on a page is the purchase. Labels sentence case, verb first: *Add to cart*,
*Compare models*. Disabled is 35 % opacity and the label becomes the reason (*Sold out*).
