Birthed in Britain, Wishbone Golf merges exquisite design with unwavering quality.

Unchanged in v2 — the words were always right; the brand just looks like them now.

Do
  • Name the material — aircraft-grade aluminium, sealed bearings
  • Say the number — 27+ holes, 799 €, 05 models
  • Report, don't sell: “Range · 27+ holes” beats “Incredible battery life”
  • Print — for a value you do not have
  • British spelling throughout
Don't
  • Innovative · premium · game-changing
  • Exclamation marks, emoji
  • Invent a spec to fill a panel
  • “aluminum” inside British copy
  • Two loud things on one screen

Naming: Wishbone ONE, Wishbone EON — brand first, model in caps. Panels are numbered 01, 02, 03 in reading order; the numbers are addresses, not decoration.
